    How your matches are checked and ranked

    First: added within 24 hours. Second: added within 3 days. Third: older listings. Within each group, refurbishment mentions and budget fit receive a boost. One-bed target £1,800, maximum £2,000; two-bed maximum £2,100. A floor plan is required.

    The radius is exactly 1 mile, using Rightmove’s unrounded Denmark Hill station distance where available, or distance from its station map point. Rightmove’s pins can be approximate: uncertain homes appear under Needs checking. Station screening excludes homes at or within 0.1 miles of any listed station. Railway screening excludes pins within 50 m of tracks and named streets running alongside tracks within 50 m for at least 50 m. These map checks help screen listings; they cannot guarantee a quiet home. Refurbishment badges quote the advertiser’s description. Street and railway geometry: © OpenStreetMap contributors.
